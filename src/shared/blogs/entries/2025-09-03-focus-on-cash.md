# Focus on Cash

File Size: 8262

You can now work for cash. There's even a method to render dynamic pixelated
text on a single line to display the caish you've worked for. You can't spend
it yet, but we'll get there.

You can also zoom in on your furry friends, who can look left and right. I have
no idea why it's so hard to implement a basic zooming camera every time, but
here we are. The order: translate to focus point, rotate and scale, translate
to world point (where you are focusing in world coordinates). Thank you Colf
for having already conquered this issue (sort of).
