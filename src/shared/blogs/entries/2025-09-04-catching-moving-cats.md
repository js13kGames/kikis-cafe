# Your Move, Cats!

File Size: 9027

As in, cats can now walk around. They mostly seem to wander off the screen, the
little idiots, so I'll have to work on that. Both bounds to keep them from
living in the sky, and some sort of system to handle them being "away". Still,
it's nice to finally have some movement in the game.

You can also catch cats now. There's zero challenge to it yet, so it's not very
fun, but it's a nice step. I was having a nice time using rokay forms to handle
a bit of the UI, but I've found a bug in rokay form model generation that's
actually quite hard to get around. Editable, optional, form-model-having fields
don't seem to generate quite right. It's rather scuttled my plans to start
deploying this, unfortunately. Perhaps a rokay 0.0.39 will be in the near
future...

I'm also starting to record daily builds in the repo. It's far enough along that
it might be interesting to look back on them.
